Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 and about 200 hours in one playthrough. There are no difficulty settings, no mission replay and no New Game+. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off your platinum save.

⚠ The one trophy you can miss: MISSABLE Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot. Do the balcony meditation with Lucia at Jason's house in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth. It cannot be done again after Chapter 1.

Saves: 15 manual slots + 1 autosave. Keep a separate save at the start and end of every chapter — that is 10 slots, and you can rotate the other 5 in free roam after the ending.

Avoid: the Irredeemable criminal profile — it is permanent and makes the police more aggressive.
